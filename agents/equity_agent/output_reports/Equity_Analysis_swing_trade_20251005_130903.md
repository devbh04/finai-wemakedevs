## Investment Implementation Strategy

### Option 1 – One‑Time Lump‑Sum (Stocks)

- **Total Amount**: **₹1,50,339**  
- **Recommended For**: Investors with immediate cash ready for swing‑trade; aligns with medium‑risk, 3‑5 yr horizon.  

**Implementation Steps**
1. **Week 1** – Open a demat account (if not already) and place market orders for the five stocks as per allocation above.  
2. **Week 2** – Review execution prices; if any stock’s price deviates > 2 % from the quoted price, consider a limit order to improve entry.  
3. **Week 3** – Set up price alerts for each holding to manage swing‑trade exits (target 8‑12 % upside, stop‑loss 5 %).  

### Option 2 – Systematic Investment Plan (Mutual Funds)

- **Total Monthly SIP**: **₹44,768** (distributed as per table).  
- **Recommended For**: Regular income earners seeking rupee‑cost averaging and reduced timing risk.  

**Implementation Steps**
1. **Day 1 of each month** – Initiate SIP through the chosen AMC’s online portal or via your bank’s auto‑debit.  
2. **Month 1‑12** – Monitor NAVs; if any fund’s NAV spikes > 15 % month‑on‑month, consider pausing that fund’s SIP for a month.  
3. **Quarterly** – Review portfolio performance against benchmarks; rebalance if any fund’s weight deviates > 5 % from target.  

### Option 3 – Hybrid Approach (Recommended)

- **Lump‑Sum**: **₹1,50,339** into the five stocks (as above).  
- **Monthly SIP**: **₹44,768** into the five mutual funds (as above).  

*This combines the upside potential of swing‑trade equity with the stability and compounding benefits of diversified mutual funds.*

## Risk Management & Monitoring

- **Review Frequency**: Quarterly portfolio review; monthly SIP performance check.  
- **Rebalancing Triggers**:  
  - Any single stock exceeds 30 % of equity allocation.  
  - Fund weight deviates > 5 % from target allocation.  
  - DTI rises above 45 % (if new loan taken).  
- **Exit Strategy**:  
  - **Stocks** – Set predefined swing‑trade targets (8‑12 % upside) and stop‑loss (5 %). Exit fully if target hit or fundamentals deteriorate.  
  - **Mutual Funds** – Hold for minimum 3 years; consider redemption if fund underperforms its benchmark by > 2 % over 12 months.
