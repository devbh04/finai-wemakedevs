| **Week 4** | Invest **₹ 10,64,000** each into the five listed mutual‑funds (buy at current NAVs). |
| **Week 5** | Keep remaining cash as a buffer for the first month’s SIP and emergency‑fund top‑up. |

#### Option 2 – Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) – Ongoing

- **Start Date:** 1st of next month after lump‑sum execution.  
- **Frequency:** Monthly, on the **5th** of each month.  
- **Allocation:** ₹ 60,000 per fund (total ₹ 3,00,000).  
- **Execution:** Set up auto‑debit from the business’s current account to each fund’s SIP portal.

#### Option 3 – Hybrid Approach (Recommended)

- **Lump‑Sum:** **₹ 25,00,000** (as detailed in Option 1) – primarily to acquire the five stocks and initial mutual‑fund units.  
- **SIP:** **₹ 3,00,000** per month (Option 2) – to build the mutual‑fund holdings over time, smooth market entry, and maintain liquidity.

**Implementation Timeline Overview**

| Month | Milestone |
